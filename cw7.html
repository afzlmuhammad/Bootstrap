More details

Tripsync is a trip planning platform built around one shared, live itinerary instead of the scattered chats, screenshots, and spreadsheets most group trips end up buried in. Travelers start by creating a trip and setting their destinations and dates — whether it's a single-city getaway, a flexible-dates trip still coming together, or a multi-city adventure — and Tripsync automatically lays out a day-by-day framework to build on. From there, they invite companions with a single link, and everyone gets real-time access to the same plan with role-based permissions for owners, editors, and viewers, so there's no more juggling who has the latest version. The group builds out the itinerary together using drag-and-drop scheduling across days and times, adding flights, hotels, activities, and reservations complete with notes, links, prices, and confirmation numbers, while a map view helps spot inefficient routing before it becomes a problem on the ground. Manual entry gets minimized too: forwarding confirmation emails lets Tripsync automatically detect and parse booking details straight into the timeline. Built-in polls and comment threads help groups actually agree on restaurants, activities, and accommodations instead of hashing it out across five different apps, and shared notes and packing lists keep everyone on the same page before departure. Once the trip starts, the itinerary works fully offline with a daily view of what's next, plus real-time alerts if a flight time or reservation changes and local weather and time zone info for each stop — making Tripsync just as useful with no signal as it is back home during the planning phase.
